# Follow-ups

## Fixes

- **Generated courses after movement replacement:** Portal Movement, Relay, and Choices still have route geometry/timings and completion assertions tuned for the old instant walk/sprint model. Their route tests fail with persistent velocity. Retune them in stage 4, after the Hotel playtest, editor integration, and the user's Obby edits; retain completion and negative-route assertions meanwhile.

- **Navigation search exhaustion:** a connected route whose A* search itself exceeds the per-query budget can still fail repeatedly, including some long Hotel detours. Continue these searches across ticks while preserving the shared work cap. Exhausting optional smoothing now retains the route already found.

- **Stuck forward movement:** the player sometimes keeps walking after W is released, during ordinary play with no focus change. `input_movement_system` rebuilds the intent from `ButtonInput<KeyCode>` every frame and the new controller brakes after release. If sustained movement continues beyond that braking period, trace whether a key release reaches `ButtonInput`. Capture a session with `WAYLAND_DEBUG=client` and check whether `wl_keyboard.key` delivered the release (key 17, state 0): if not, the loss is below the game (key remapper, keyboard, or compositor); if so, trace winit and Bevy next.

## Enhancements

- **Player stair stepping:** the movement replacement retains the shared capsule motor's existing step limit. Matching the scaled Source 0.45 m step needs an explicit capsule-aware step solver; increasing Rapier's autostep height alone stalled a 0.4 m step in the regression fixture. Keep actor traversal unchanged and check overhead clearance, narrow steps, and carrier contacts if extending player stepping.

- **Common crate scope:** `common` should hold only what both the client and server run. The player step, its controller, and the funnel policy are owner-side code the server never calls (the headless experiment reaches them through the client's planner); move such code to the client unless the editor's PyO3 preview needs it without the Bevy client crate, and audit the rest of `common` the same way. The preview now needs `player_control.rs`, `player_flight.rs`, `falling.rs`, and the portal funnel and traversal, so those stay.

- **Missile search cleanup:** every neighbour edge in `client/src/missiles/search.rs` repeats its node's start-overlap query through `sweep_clear`, about a third of the search budget; judge edges by travel alone and charge one query. `AirGraph::endpoint_candidates` probes every carrier grid at any distance; apply the reach gate `neighbors` uses. `MissileFlight.route_status`, `RouteStatus`, and `SearchBudget.used` are written in production and read only by tests, and `AirGraph::path` is a test helper in the production file.

- **Dependency upgrades:** Recheck the `encase` family held at 0.12.1 in `Cargo.lock` once [Bevy's syn compatibility issue](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/issues/25844) is resolved; 0.12.2 fails to compile with Bevy 0.19.1. Renet's `crypto-common` dependency also pins `generic-array` to 0.14.7.

- **Render ramps as stairs:** add an option to show ramps as stairs while retaining smooth ramp collision and movement. Make stair use configurable per actor kind, like ladder use.

- **Surface navigation:** Plan detours larger than one navigation window globally; a search that exceeds its own query budget is the Fix above. Bake in tiles: a field change still rebuilds a whole region over seconds while the previous mesh serves, and Hotel's preloaded region sits near the four-million-column bake limit, which a larger grid or roam extension would exceed. Improve recovery after interrupted boarding/climbing and support removal: a mount, climb, exit, or boarding that another body blocks only holds, and its stall replans from a position off the mesh. Standoffs end only between two movers that block each other; a longer cycle, or a mover behind an immovable body in a passage too narrow to pass, still waits. Transfers connect a carrier to its parent only at authored motion endpoints, so arbitrary meetings between moving carriers, mid-rung attacks, and actor portal routes remain outstanding. Track compiled collision geometry back to authored objects, and add ordinary actor route/status inspection and experiment reset tools.

- **World-space content authoring:** support placing items, actor zones, objectives, and authored geometry throughout the playable world, including the surrounding terrain. The authored grid boundary should bound an editing region, not gameplay eligibility; use shared collision/support and traversal rules for content inside and outside it. Navigation already loads regions beyond the grid.

- **Pressure-plate sizing:** allow plate size and activation area to be authored independently of grid resolution. Portal Relay uses a 2 m grid with 1 m plates; broader landing targets should not require coarser geometry.

- **Funnel independent of input:** the portal funnel should draw a body in whether or not movement keys are held. It still acts only on released input, because a pull that ignores input pins a steering player inside a floor-to-ceiling fall loop, which steering must be able to leave. Give the pull a strength steering can beat, then drop the input condition in `player_step.rs` and in the editor preview's flight step.

- **Editor portal fit guidance:** add backing-size and front-clearance checks across adjoining cells. Jump Path places a floor portal at any point and judges it by the cell under its centre alone, so an aperture reaching over a ledge, a ramp, or a resistant neighbour still reads as placeable.

- **Editor preview scope:** Jump Path flies open air through `map_core::preview` at full speed from a point on a tile edge. Show the run-up: the ground-acceleration distance a standing start needs before the edge, with the takeoff choosing a standing or running start; a step also leaves its edge at the grid line, not where the capsule loses support. Let obstacles, ramps, and light bridges stop a flight: a landing is judged only by the floor slab under it, and walls are flown through. Add crouch (tilted portal exits force it in the game), ramp takeoffs, mid-flight equipment changes (erasers, pickups), gravity changes during a flight, switches, and carriers. Steering is input held in one direction for the whole flight, which understates what changing input in the air reaches, and a steered entry aims by one fixed rule; search the input instead. Outline where a wall portal catches a flight, as floor portals have. The user retunes Obby by hand with this tool; generated maps follow afterward. See [the staged handoff](EXPERIMENTS.md#current-handoff-movement-first-hotel-playtest).

- **Sandbox simulation and experiment workflow:** build toward AI-generated, complex movement and portal maps that are fun to play; [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md) records the goals, design constraints, and current evidence. Extend the `--experiment` runner with carrier simulation, route search and landing-tolerance measurements, plus seeded randomness for reproducible traces with actors. Separate scenes, rulesets, and repeatable experiment setups; preserve client authority for local movement and server authority for actors and synchronization, without movement reconciliation. Prove reusable body, controller, team, and loadout composition in two different experiments, then connect runtime inspection and reset to the existing editor. Introduce reusable world conditions/actions and explicit state lifetimes as those experiments need them.

- **Rapier upgrades:** Recheck the capsule floor-motion regression before removing the contact-normal adapter in `common/src/physics/world/character_queries.rs`. It works around imprecise convex cast normals feeding Rapier’s slope decomposition; triangle-mesh casts retain their own normals because a neighboring triangle contact can otherwise stall terrain movement. `running_across_flat_floor_tiles_keeps_its_speed` still fails without it on 0.35. Whatever replaces it must keep its contact query bounded, since an unbounded prediction scans the whole terrain trimesh.

## Testing

- **Air-control playtest:** Hotel inherits matching ground/air acceleration, deceleration, and lateral deceleration of 20/30/40 m/s². Check released-input stopping, direction changes, low-gravity jumps, speed pickups, and portal funneling. Obby explicitly disables all three air rates; verify its takeoff and portal trajectories without manual air steering. `move_speed_power_up` remains a multiplier of horizontal speed only. Automated coverage checks units, independent braking, zero-rate momentum, gravity, blast decay, and funnel capture; human feel still needs confirmation.

- **Jump Path check:** in the editor, mark a roof edge in Hotel and compare a few landings, capture outlines, and a floor-to-wall flight with the game: jump and step, each power-up selection, Air control on and off, a floor portal just inside and just outside an outline, and Obby, whose zero air rates collapse the steering outlines onto the landings. Automated tests hold the flight to the game's step, funnel, and hop tick for tick and the outlines to the flights; whether the picture reads well, and whether the legend row fits beside long map and level names, needs eyes.

- **Graphical experiment playtest:** play and step through Portal Relay with `--play-experiment`, inspect portals and airborne motion from different camera angles, and check the overlay, Escape menu, and restart in the actual window. Automated tests cover continuous and single-action playback, frame-rate-independent results, paused gameplay, camera/input isolation, Escape menu/cursor controls, and renderer state restoration after death and restart.

- **Portal Relay playtest:** play with `--map portal_relay`; assess route discovery, visibility of the offset lower pads, third-person portal aiming at ledge lips, landing on the smaller pressure plates, jump and air-steering comfort, and checkpoint pacing. Check editor responsiveness with the 2 m grid and doubled level count. Headless tests cover completion, grounded portal shots across the ledge gaps, bridge activation, air-steering timing variation, and recovery after a missed landing.

- **Portal Choices playtest:** play `--map portal_choices` and its graphical experiment replay; verify the final approach with its revised 22 m gap, and assess both lower pads' visibility from the approach. The player confirmed making the final approach by sprinting; ordinary sprinting and the speed pickup are distinct, and the latest spacing still needs manual confirmation. Check whether the three ramps facing one finish make their trajectories understandable and whether the speed refill and retry deck are easy to find. Look for alternative portal placements and air steering that bypass the intended decisions. Automated checks cover the full course, grounded pad shots with at most 60 degrees of downward aim, the final approach with all speed pickups removed (walking jumps, varied sprint takeoffs, and a missed jump), direct shortcuts, required boosts, blocked setup shots, alternative arcs above the shared finish, approach-timing tolerance, overshooting without erasure even with countersteering, checkpoint respawns, and completing another speed launch after losing the boost on the approach, ramp, or upper platform.

- **Client playtest:** check crowded ledges, ramps, and moving supports in Hotel/Obby; missile pursuit through moving rooms and around exterior obstacles with several simultaneous launches; and grass streaming, explosion recovery, and low plank clearance. Headless regression and budget tests cover these systems; visual frame pacing and appearance still need an in-game pass.
